ABOUT DE ANDISA

Built around reliability, responsiveness and professional execution.

De Andisa Optimum Services Ltd. is a Nigerian-owned support services company serving procurement, engineering, oil & gas, marine, logistics and general supply requirements.

We support clients by identifying suitable products, service providers and operational solutions, coordinating requirements and maintaining clear communication from enquiry through delivery.

Our approach is practical: understand the requirement, source responsibly, document clearly and support timely execution.
